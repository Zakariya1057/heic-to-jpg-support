Privacy policy · Effective 19 October 2026

Your documents stay
under your control.

PDF to Word is developed by Zakariya. It converts selected PDFs into DOCX documents using Apple’s on-device frameworks, including local text recognition. There is no app backend, account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking or remote conversion service. The developer does not receive your documents through the app.

Documents and recognition

The app processes PDFs you choose through the system Files picker or drag into the Mac app. It creates a private local input copy and converts text, recognized document structure and detected raster illustrations into a new DOCX. Recognition runs on your device. Original files are not modified. Formatting and recognition can change or omit content; keep your original and review the output.

Storage and deletion

Input copies and conversion results are held in an app-owned temporary workspace. Clearing the queue, removing failed items or discarding unsaved conversions attempts to delete those private copies. Cleanup is also attempted when the workspace is destroyed. Crashes, force-quitting and operating-system behavior can leave temporary files; immediate or secure deletion is not promised.

Outputs saved through Files or to a Mac folder remain in that destination when you clear the queue or uninstall the app. Manage or delete those documents in Files, Finder or the selected storage provider. Backups and synchronized copies follow your chosen services’ behavior.

File access and sharing

Access is scoped to sources and destinations you select through native system interfaces. The app does not request Photos access. It does not use a document-upload service or remote recognition SDK.

iCloud and other file providers may download selected originals or synchronize saved outputs according to your settings. A service receives a document when you explicitly save or share it there. Those services have their own policies; the developer does not receive your document through this app.

Purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles App Store payments; the app does not collect payment-card details. No remote analytics or crash-reporting service is included in the app. Apple may process usage, crash or transaction information according to its policies and your system settings.

Support

You can email zakaria2011@live.no for support. The developer and the email services involved receive the information you send, including your email address and message. Describe the error and your device/operating-system version; please do not send PDFs, document contents or sensitive personal information.

If you instead contact support through GitHub, the developer and GitHub receive the information you submit. Issues are public. Do not upload PDFs, document contents, private file paths or sensitive personal information. GitHub’s privacy policy applies to that service.

This website

This static website is hosted on Vercel. It has no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or document uploads and sets no application cookies. Vercel processes standard web-request information, such as IP address, user-agent and requested URL, to deliver and secure the site; hosting logs may be available to the operator. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice. Opening GitHub follows GitHub’s Privacy Statement; viewing this site alone sends no request to GitHub.

Questions and changes

Email support for privacy questions. This policy will be updated if app practices change; the date above identifies this version.
