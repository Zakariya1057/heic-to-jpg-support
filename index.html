Use a result’s three-dot menu to open or share it. On Mac, Show in Finder reveals the saved file. Your originals stay unchanged.

Common questions

A clear next step.

Why does the app say my photo isn’t HEIC?

The app checks the image’s actual format. The Photos picker can also show JPEGs and PNGs, which this HEIC converter cannot convert. Choose an original HEIC or HEIF photo; changing a filename to .heic does not change its format. Unsupported or damaged images cannot be fixed by retrying.

Where did my converted image go?

On iPhone or iPad, it goes to the Photos library or Files destination you chose. On Mac, it goes into your chosen folder; use Show in Finder to find it. Output files use the .jpeg extension. Mac export adds a number if the name is already taken rather than overwriting a file.

Why does saving to Photos need permission?

Saving a new image needs permission to add to Photos. The app asks for add-only access, not permission to browse your entire library. If access is denied or restricted, choose Save to Files in the error message. You can also review the permission in device Settings.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancelling a save choice or destination dialog discards that new unsaved conversion and its private temporary copies. Previously saved images stay where you saved them. Cancelling conversion stops at a safe boundary; unfinished queued work can be retried.

Does it work without the internet?

Conversion runs on your device. If an original is stored only in iCloud or another file provider, the system may first need to download it. Saving to a cloud destination or using a sharing service can also need a connection.

Are my location and camera details kept?

Conversion strips camera and location metadata while preserving orientation and image dimensions. JPEG is a lossy format, encoded at 90% quality. The app converts the primary still image; depth, animation, Live Photo video and HDR gain maps are not included.

Is there a batch or file-size limit?

A batch holds up to 50 images, with a 50 MiB limit per source and 200 MiB total source data. Decoded images are limited to 100 megapixels. Remove failed inputs or clear the queue to make room. Large images can take longer to convert.

A conversion failed. What should I try?

Use Retry unfinished photos for queued work or a potentially temporary conversion failure. For an unsupported, damaged or over-limit image, choose a valid original instead. Check that you have storage space, that cloud originals are downloaded and that your save destination is writable.
